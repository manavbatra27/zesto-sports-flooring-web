Why this page exists

Not simply a bag of coloured rubber.

For a sports or play-surface contractor, the granule is one component inside a complete installed system. Surface appearance, site productivity and finished performance depend on choosing the right granule for the application and coordinating it with the binder, base, layer build-up and installation method.

Where contractors use EPDM

One material family. Multiple project environments.

EPDM granules are commonly specified as the coloured wearing layer in resilient sports, recreation and safety surfacing systems. The exact system build-up should follow the project specification and site conditions.

Colour wearing layers for track, warm-up, jogging and training environments where appearance and repeatable site execution matter.

Recreation & activity zones

Outdoor fitness, school recreation, circulation and amenity areas where a resilient coloured surface is specified.

What to evaluate

Granule selection is a technical and visual decision.

  • Colour and batch planning
    Important on larger visible areas and multi-phase projects.
  • Particle-size distribution
    Should suit the specified system and intended surface texture.
  • Binder-to-granule system
    Confirm the complete installation system with the binder and system supplier / designer.
  • Base and drainage readiness
    A high-quality wearing layer cannot compensate for an unsuitable or poorly prepared substrate.
  • Site temperature and working conditions
    Execution planning should reflect the binder system and actual site environment.
